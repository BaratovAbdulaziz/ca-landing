Counter Arena / identity exploration

Four ways forward.

Each concept uses the new athlete reference and the current Counter Arena wordmark. These are options for review; the live logo has not changed.

01 / MONOGRAM

Athlete + A

A bold A form holds the portrait and makes the mark feel closest to the current identity.

02 / ARENA

Competition badge

An angular frame gives the character a more traditional sports team identity.

03 / MOTION

Push-up mark

The exercise itself leads the logo, with a blue chevron anchoring the movement.

04 / PORTRAIT

Character mark

The simplest mascot option keeps the face prominent and the framing minimal.
